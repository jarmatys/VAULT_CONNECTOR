# Garden co-op plan

The co-op shares twelve raised beds behind the community hall. Goals for the season:

- Rotate tomatoes and beans between beds 1 to 6, so no bed grows tomatoes two years running.
- Install a rain barrel by the shed before April.
- Hold the seed swap on the first Saturday of March.

Budget: 400 credits from the members' fund, of which 150 go to the rain barrel.
